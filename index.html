About

Lava Wu (She/Her) is a mixed media artist and writer working on psychology, humanity, and social topics that convey empathy, love, spiritual development, and sustainability.

She takes commissions worldwide across both fine art and commercial work — regardless of form, she always aims to portray emotion and create an atmosphere that connects with the audience.
